Privacy notice

Last updated: 1 December 2026

This notice explains how I collect, use and protect personal information when you contact me, when you use my services and when you visit this website. I take the confidentiality of the information you share with me very seriously, both as a legal obligation and as part of my professional responsibilities as a psychologist.

Who I am

NSL Psychology is the trading name of Nethali Sabbah Lerner, Clinical and Counselling Psychologist, registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (registration no. PYL36914). I am the data controller for the personal information described in this notice.

The information I collect

  • When you get in touch: your name, contact details and the brief outline of the support or assessment you are looking for.
  • If you become a client: information needed to provide therapy or an assessment, including details about your health, development, education, family and personal circumstances, and session notes, test results and reports.
  • For children and young people: information provided by the young person, their parents or carers and, with consent, their school or other professionals.
  • For payment and insurance: billing details and, if you use private health insurance, your insurer, membership number and any authorisation details.

Information about your health is "special category" information under data protection law, and I apply additional safeguards to it.

Why I use your information, and the legal basis

  • Responding to your enquiry: my legitimate interest in replying to people who contact me, or steps taken at your request before entering into an agreement.
  • Providing therapy and assessments: to perform my agreement with you. Health information is processed for the purposes of health or social care, under the duty of professional confidentiality.
  • Keeping financial records: to meet my legal obligations, for example for tax purposes.
  • Safeguarding: where there is a serious risk of harm to you or to someone else, I may need to share information without consent, as permitted or required by law and by my professional standards.

Who I share your information with

I do not sell your information or use it for marketing. I share it only where necessary, and usually only with your consent:

  • your insurer, if you choose to use private health insurance;
  • your GP, school or other professionals involved in your care, with your consent;
  • services I use to run the practice, such as secure email and record-keeping systems, a payment provider and an accountant, which may only use the information on my instructions;
  • others where the law requires it, for example in response to a court order or to protect someone from serious harm.

Some of these services may store information outside the United Kingdom.

How long I keep your information

I keep clinical records for as long as is necessary to provide care and to meet my professional and legal obligations, in line with professional guidance and the NHS Records Management Code of Practice. Records for children and young people are kept for longer than those for adults. Financial records are kept for the period required by law. Enquiries that do not lead to an appointment are deleted within a reasonable period. When records are no longer needed, they are securely destroyed.

How I protect your information

Records are kept securely, with access restricted to me. Electronic records are password-protected and stored on secure systems, and paper records, where used, are kept locked away. Please be aware that ordinary email is not fully secure; in your first message, please give only a brief outline, and we can discuss the details later.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the information I hold about you, to ask me to correct it, and in some circumstances to ask me to delete it, restrict its use or object to its use. Parents and carers may make requests on behalf of a child, taking into account the young person's age and understanding. To make a request, please contact me using the details above. I will respond within one month.

This website and cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. With your agreement, it uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed and how people arrive at the site. Google Analytics sets cookies on your device and shares information about your visit, including your IP address, with Google. It is only switched on if you choose "Accept analytics", and you can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" at the bottom of each page. If you reject analytics, no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is remembered in your browser's storage so that you are not asked again on each visit.

The map on the main page is provided by Google Maps, so your browser connects to Google's servers when the page loads, which shares your IP address with Google, and Google may set its own cookies. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's privacy policy. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep server logs for security purposes.

Complaints

If you have any concerns about how I handle your information, please contact me first and I will do my best to resolve them. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

I may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
