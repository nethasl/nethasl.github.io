EMDR – trauma-focused therapy

Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing

EMDR is an evidence-based psychological therapy that helps people to process and recover from traumatic or distressing experiences. When difficult experiences have not been fully processed, they can continue to affect emotions, thoughts, behaviour and physical wellbeing, sometimes long after the event itself.

EMDR helps the brain to process these memories in a way that reduces their emotional intensity, allowing you to move forward without feeling overwhelmed by the past. I offer EMDR to adults, adolescents and children aged 11 and above.

What to expect

Therapy begins with an assessment and preparation phase to ensure that EMDR is appropriate for your needs. The number of sessions depends on the nature of the difficulties being addressed, and the work always proceeds at a pace that feels safe for you.

I have extensive experience of working with PTSD, complex trauma and trauma-related difficulties, including those arising from:

  • Accidents and life-threatening experiences
  • Physical or sexual assault, abuse and domestic violence
  • Childhood trauma, neglect and adverse experiences
  • Bereavement and traumatic loss
  • Medical trauma, difficult childbirth and birth-related experiences
  • Witnessing traumatic events or experiencing secondary trauma
  • Terrorism, violence and other critical incidents

Psychological & neurodevelopmental assessments

Understanding strengths, difficulties and needs

Assessments can help individuals, families and schools to understand a person's cognitive abilities, attention, learning profile and developmental needs, and the support that would be most helpful. I assess children, adolescents and adults.

Each assessment combines standardised tests with interviews and input from family, school or other professionals, and concludes with a detailed written report and practical recommendations.

ADHD assessments

A comprehensive assessment of attention, concentration, impulsivity, hyperactivity and executive functioning, using clinical interviews, developmental history and standardised questionnaires such as the Conners-4, to establish whether diagnostic criteria for ADHD are met and to identify appropriate support.

Cognitive assessments (IQ testing)

Standardised assessment, using the WISC-V and other age-appropriate measures, of verbal comprehension, visual-spatial reasoning, working memory and processing speed. The findings help to clarify learning needs and inform support at home, at school or in further education.

Autism Spectrum Condition (ASC) assessments

Specialist autism assessments for children, adolescents and adults, using the ADOS-2 and a detailed developmental history, drawing on my experience in NHS and private diagnostic teams.

Consultation and support for schools

Consultation and assessment for schools, with practical guidance on pupils' learning, attention, emotional and developmental needs, and the adjustments that can help.

Where my experience comes from

Much of my specialist interest in trauma grew out of work abroad. In Madrid, I supported children, adults and bereaved families affected by the terrorist attacks of 11 March 2004, helping them through grief and the aftermath of a traumatic event. In Kigali, Rwanda, with the Service de Consultations Psychosociales, I worked with survivors of the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi and with people affected by sexual violence. That experience of complex trauma, profound loss and severe distress has shaped how I work ever since.

In London, much of my work has been with children, young people and families. At Kids Company I supported highly vulnerable young people facing neglect, abuse, domestic violence, bereavement and attachment difficulties. At Children's Therapies, a private multidisciplinary clinic, I offered assessment and therapy for anxiety, low mood, attachment difficulties and post-traumatic stress. At Newham CAMHS' Children's Development Centre (East London NHS Foundation Trust), I carried out autism assessments for children aged 2 to 18, alongside wider CAMHS work.

I have also served as a member of the French Embassy's Psychological Response Team, supporting people affected by crises and emergencies, and worked as an associate psychologist with PsychWorks, providing consultancy and medico-legal work around trauma, adjustment to illness and disability, and family relationships.
