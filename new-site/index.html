About me

Experienced, compassionate and collaborative

I am an experienced and compassionate Clinical Psychologist with over 17 years of experience working across the NHS, voluntary and private sectors, supporting children aged 11 and above, adolescents, adults and families experiencing a wide range of emotional, psychological, behavioural and mental health difficulties.

I aim to provide a warm, empathic and supportive therapeutic environment where individuals feel safe to explore their thoughts, feelings and experiences openly, without fear of judgement. I believe that developing a trusting and collaborative therapeutic relationship is central to meaningful psychological change.

I am passionate about helping young people and adults develop a deeper understanding of themselves, navigate life's challenges and build on their existing strengths and resources. My approach is grounded in the belief that therapy should offer a safe and nurturing space in which difficult experiences and emotions can be explored and processed, while supporting individuals to develop practical and sustainable ways of moving forward.

As an integrative practitioner, I draw on a range of evidence-based psychological approaches, adapting therapy to each individual's needs, circumstances and goals. My work incorporates Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing (EMDR), Compassion Focused Therapy (CFT), narrative and systemic approaches, alongside other therapeutic interventions. I have a particular interest and extensive experience in working with trauma and its impact on emotional wellbeing, relationships and development.

Alongside my private practice, I work part-time within the NHS in a specialist Child and Adolescent Mental Health Service (CAMHS), supporting young people and families experiencing complex mental health difficulties.

In addition to psychological therapy, I offer specialist psychological assessments, including ADHD and cognitive assessments, to help children, young people and families better understand their individual strengths, difficulties and support needs.

I offer psychological consultations and therapy in English, French and Spanish.

EMDR – trauma-focused therapy

Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing

I offer Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing (EMDR) therapy to adults, adolescents and children aged 11 and above.

EMDR is an evidence-based psychological therapy designed to help individuals process and recover from traumatic or distressing life experiences. When difficult experiences have not been fully processed, they can continue to affect our emotions, thoughts, behaviours and physical wellbeing, sometimes long after the event itself.

EMDR helps the brain process these memories in a way that reduces their emotional intensity and allows individuals to move forward without feeling overwhelmed by the past.

What to expect from EMDR therapy

EMDR begins with a comprehensive assessment and preparation phase to ensure that the approach is appropriate for your individual needs. Treatment is tailored to each person, and the number of sessions will depend on the nature and complexity of the difficulties being addressed.

My approach is compassionate, collaborative and sensitive to each individual's pace, ensuring that you feel supported and safe throughout the therapeutic process.

I have extensive clinical experience working with Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD), complex trauma and trauma-related psychological difficulties, including those arising from:

  • Accidents and life-threatening experiences
  • Physical or sexual assault, abuse and domestic violence
  • Childhood trauma, neglect and adverse experiences
  • Bereavement and traumatic loss
  • Medical trauma, difficult childbirth and birth-related experiences
  • Witnessing traumatic events or experiencing secondary trauma
  • Terrorism, violence and other critical incidents

Psychological & neurodevelopmental assessments

Understanding strengths, difficulties and needs

I offer specialist psychological and neurodevelopmental assessments for children, adolescents and adults, helping individuals and families gain a better understanding of their cognitive abilities, attention, learning profiles and developmental needs.

My assessments combine standardised psychological measures with comprehensive clinical interviews, developmental histories and information gathered from families, schools and other relevant professionals. Each assessment is tailored to the individual's needs and includes a detailed written report outlining the findings and practical recommendations.

Attention

ADHD assessments

Comprehensive assessments exploring attention, concentration, impulsivity, hyperactivity and executive functioning difficulties. Assessments include clinical interviews, developmental history and standardised questionnaires, such as the Conners-4, to establish whether an individual meets diagnostic criteria for ADHD and identify appropriate support strategies.

Learning

Cognitive assessments (IQ testing)

Standardised cognitive assessments to identify individual strengths and difficulties across areas such as verbal comprehension, visual-spatial reasoning, working memory and processing speed.

These assessments can help clarify learning needs, inform educational support and provide recommendations for home, school or further education. Assessment tools include the WISC-V and other age-appropriate cognitive measures.

Neurodevelopment

Autism Spectrum Condition (ASC) assessments

I have extensive experience conducting specialist autism assessments for children, adolescents and adults within NHS and private multidisciplinary diagnostic pathways.

My expertise includes the Autism Diagnostic Observation Schedule, Second Edition (ADOS-2), and comprehensive developmental history assessments, contributing to a detailed understanding of an individual's social communication, developmental profile and support needs.

Education

Consultation and support for schools

I also offer specialist psychological consultation and assessment services to schools, providing guidance on children's cognitive, attentional, emotional and developmental needs.

Recommendations are tailored to support learning, emotional wellbeing and appropriate educational adjustments.
