EMDR – trauma-focused therapy

Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing

EMDR is a well-researched therapy that helps people recover from traumatic or distressing experiences. When a difficult memory hasn't been fully processed, it can keep affecting how you feel, think and react, sometimes long after the event.

EMDR helps the brain reprocess these memories so they lose their intensity, and you can move forward without feeling overwhelmed by the past. I offer EMDR to young people (11+) and adults.

What to expect

We start with an assessment and some preparation, to make sure EMDR is right for you. The number of sessions depends on what we're working on, and we'll always go at a pace that feels safe.

I have extensive experience helping people with PTSD and complex trauma, including after:

  • Accidents and life-threatening experiences
  • Physical or sexual assault, abuse and domestic violence
  • Childhood trauma, neglect and adverse experiences
  • Bereavement and traumatic loss
  • Medical trauma, difficult childbirth and birth-related experiences
  • Witnessing traumatic events or experiencing secondary trauma
  • Terrorism, violence and other critical incidents

Psychological & neurodevelopmental assessments

Understanding strengths, difficulties and needs

An assessment can help you, your child or your school understand how someone thinks, learns and pays attention, and what support will help most. I assess children, adolescents and adults.

Each assessment combines standardised tests with interviews and input from family, school or other professionals, and ends with a clear written report and practical recommendations.

Attention

ADHD assessments

A detailed look at attention, concentration, impulsivity, hyperactivity and organisation, using interviews, developmental history and questionnaires such as the Conners-4. The aim is to establish whether ADHD criteria are met and which support strategies would help.

Learning

Cognitive assessments (IQ testing)

Standardised testing, such as the WISC-V, of verbal reasoning, visual-spatial skills, working memory and processing speed. The results clarify strengths and learning needs, and guide support at home, school or college.

Neurodevelopment

Autism Spectrum Condition (ASC) assessments

Specialist autism assessments for children, adolescents and adults, using the ADOS-2 and a detailed developmental history, drawing on my experience in NHS and private diagnostic teams.

Education

Consultation and support for schools

Consultation and assessment for schools, with practical guidance on pupils' learning, attention, emotional and developmental needs, and the adjustments that can help.

Where my experience comes from

Much of my specialist interest in trauma grew out of work abroad. In Madrid, I supported children, adults and bereaved families affected by the terrorist attacks of 11 March 2004, helping them through grief and the aftermath of a traumatic event. In Kigali, Rwanda, with the Service de Consultations Psychosociales, I worked with survivors of the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi and with people affected by sexual violence. That experience of complex trauma, profound loss and severe distress has shaped how I work ever since.

In London, much of my work has been with children, young people and families. At Kids Company I supported highly vulnerable young people facing neglect, abuse, domestic violence, bereavement and attachment difficulties. At Children's Therapies, a private multidisciplinary clinic, I offered assessment and therapy for anxiety, low mood, attachment difficulties and post-traumatic stress. At Newham CAMHS' Children's Development Centre (East London NHS Foundation Trust), I carried out autism assessments for children aged 2 to 18, alongside wider CAMHS work.

I have also been part of the French Embassy's Psychological Response Team, supporting people affected by crises and emergencies, and worked as an associate psychologist with PsychWorks, providing consultancy and medico-legal work around trauma, adjustment to illness and disability, and family relationships.
